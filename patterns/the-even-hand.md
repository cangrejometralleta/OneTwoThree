# The Even Hand

- A Machine Reaches for Symmetry.
  It Looks finished.
- A Human Writes to the Breath,
  and Stops where the Thought Stops,
  which is rarely the same Place twice.
- Balance is not Rhythm.
  Balance is what Rhythm Looks like
  when nobody Felt it.
- We Measured this Repository.
  The flattest Sections were the Generated ones.
- Measure your own Text.
  The flattest Passage is the one you Assembled
  instead of Hearing.
- An Author Writes long, then short, then stops.
  A Generator Writes even.
