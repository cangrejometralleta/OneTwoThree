# Chaos is a Source

- Chance and serialism both Generate
  what the author would not have chosen.
- Cage Tossed the I Ching
  and let the coins compose.
- Ross Called them happy accidents
  and painted the tree the slip suggested.
- Cage Invited the chaos,
  Ross Absorbed the one that arrived.
- A machine Hallucinates the same way:
  it rolls the dice and offers a result.
- Most of it is Noise,
  a few of the small madnesses are a Door.
- Cage Kept whatever chance gave,
  you Keep only the doors.
- The system Generates, the human selects.
- Roll the Dice inside a rule,
  and the outcome is still Yours.
- The best systems Metabolize
  what they cannot predict.
