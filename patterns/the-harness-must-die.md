# The Harness must Die

- OneTwoThree is a temporary scaffold of language, patterns and rules for working with an agent.
- Keep the guidance that helps people understand and verify the work. Remove steps that have become ceremony.
- Review the scaffold by trying the work with lighter guidance. Its purpose includes making what it taught reusable without it.
