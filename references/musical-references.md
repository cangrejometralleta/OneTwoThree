# Musical References

- De La Soul ([EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Soul) · [ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_La_Soul)),
  [*Ring Ring Ring (Ha Ha Hey)* on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/6qCmReiPHtF7nf9eo8GtVp) —
  the near-emblem of the Project.
  It names [yo-yo-yo](../.agents/skills/yo-yo-yo/SKILL.md),
  the session Opening.
