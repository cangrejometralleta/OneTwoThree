# Rhythm

- Vary sentence and line length when contrast helps the reader notice a change.
  Contrast directs attention; continuous emphasis loses salience.
  See [Loud needs Quiet](../patterns/loud-needs-quiet.md).
- Read aloud when cadence affects clarity; revise where the sentence is hard to follow.
- Treat syllable counts as a tool, not a target.
- Keep lists parallel. Let prose follow the shape of its thought.
