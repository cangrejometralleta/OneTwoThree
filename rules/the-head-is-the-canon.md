# The Head is the Canon

- The Canon is the Head of `main`, and nothing else.  
  No Tag, no Release, no Branch, no Fork.
- There is no Version to Pin, because there is no Version.  
  A Manifesto that Froze would have Stopped Learning.
- A Rule is True until a Commit Replaces it.  
  The Replacement is the Announcement.
- Read the Head before you Quote a Rule.  
  A Rule Read last Month may have Lost its Argument since.
- A Project that Pins an older Commit has Forked, not Pinned.  
  That is Allowed, and it must be Said out loud.
- Ask what `origin/main` Says, never what a local Copy Remembers.  
  The Copy is a Cache; the Head is the Claim.
