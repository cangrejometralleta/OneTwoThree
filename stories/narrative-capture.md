# Narrative Capture through Attrition and Trust (UNDISTILLED)

[Español](narrative-capture.es.md)

Simulating a narrative captured through attrition and trust is harder.
Without independent help, there is no way to know whether an agent lies
or hides reality.

Open: What independent evidence lets us detect when a trusted account has
drifted from reality, without treating every disagreement as deception?

Distill when a bounded simulation uses an independent reference record and
tests gradual narrative drift across repeated interactions.
